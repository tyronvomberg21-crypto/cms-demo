Good to Know

Questions guests ask

How do I book?

WhatsApp or phone Cecilia directly — tell her your dates and how many guests, and she'll confirm availability and the best direct rate. WhatsApp {{contact.whatsapp_display}} or call 028 313 0441.

Are all rooms en-suite?

Yes — all five luxury rooms have their own en-suite bathrooms, and each is individually furnished.

Is breakfast included?

A full English breakfast is served daily in the dining room or on the veranda overlooking the pool and mountains. Confirm inclusion for your rate when you book.

When is whale season?

Southern right whales are in Walker Bay roughly June to November, with peak calving season around August–October. Hermanus is one of the best land-based whale-watching spots on earth.

Can you help arrange tours?

Gladly — your hosts are qualified national tour guides and experienced divers, and will happily arrange whale trips, shark cage diving, wine routes and more.
